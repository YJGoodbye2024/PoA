## 1. Official Abstract
"The concept of Light Triad composed of Faith in Humanity, Humanism, and Kantianism, which describes loving and beneficent orientation towards others has been recently proposed as an antithesis to the Dark Triad concept of malevolent personality. Previous studies have shown these three traits are relatively distinct, that the Light Triad as a whole has a negative relationship with the Dark Triad, and that it can predict a number of positive outcomes. The aim of the present study was to further examine the structural, construct, and predictive validity of the Light Triad on a Serbian-speaking sample (N = 502). Confirmatory factor analysis showed that the three-factor structure of the Light Triad Scale fits the data well. The results have also shown that the Light Triad is related to but distinct from the basic personality traits, especially Agreeableness. Furthermore, the Light Triad was a significant predictor of both prosocial and antisocial behaviors, even after controlling for the effects of basic personality traits. Overall, the results of the present study provide further support for the validity and utility of the Light Triad concept."

### Detailed Interpretation

The 2021 paper by Petar Lukić and Marko Živanović, "Shedding light on the Light Triad," serves as a key validation study for the Light Triad construct, a prosocial counterpart to the well-known Dark Triad of personality (Machiavellianism, narcissism, psychopathy). The Light Triad, comprising Faith in Humanity, Humanism, and Kantianism, represents a loving and beneficent orientation toward others. The primary contribution of Lukić and Živanović's research was to provide robust evidence for the structural, construct, and predictive validity of the Light Triad concept using the Light Triad Scale (LTS).

**Structural and Construct Validity:**
A core objective of the study was to confirm the psychometric soundness of the Light Triad Scale. Through confirmatory factor analysis on a sample of 502 Serbian-speaking participants, the authors demonstrated that the proposed three-factor structure (Faith in Humanity, Humanism, Kantianism) provided a good fit for the data. This finding is consistent with the original work by Kaufman et al. and has been corroborated by subsequent research, reinforcing the idea that the three traits are related yet distinct components of a larger construct.

A significant point of inquiry in personality research has been whether the Light Triad is merely the opposite of the Dark Triad or if it is a distinct construct not simply reducible to high scores on Big Five Agreeableness. Lukić and Živanović's results showed that while the Light Triad is related to Agreeableness, it is not redundant; it remains a distinct concept with unique predictive power. The paper also affirmed the negative correlation between the Light Triad and the Dark Tetrad (Dark Triad plus sadism), which supports its construct validity as a measure of prosocial tendencies.

**Predictive Validity and Utility:**
The study established the Light Triad's utility in forecasting real-world behaviors. Lukić and Živanović found that the Light Triad was a significant predictor of both prosocial and antisocial behaviors. Crucially, this predictive power held even after accounting for the influence of the basic Big Five personality traits. This indicates that the Light Triad offers unique explanatory value beyond existing personality models when it comes to benevolent and malevolent actions.

Other researchers have built upon these findings, citing Lukić and Živanović's work to underscore the Light Triad's role in various domains. For instance, the scale's validity has been referenced in studies examining long-term mating strategies, where Light Triad traits positively predict interest in such relationships. The paper is also cited in research that critiques the psychometric properties of the Light Triad Scale, suggesting that while foundational, further development may be needed to reduce measurement error in certain subscales. Overall, the paper is consistently presented in the literature as a solid piece of evidence confirming that the Light Triad is a valid and useful construct for understanding the positive aspects of human personality.
